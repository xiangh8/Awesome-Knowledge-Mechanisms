Overview

Knowledge as a continuously changing object

Knowledge is fundamental to the capabilities of large language models. Understanding how models acquire, store, use, and revise knowledge is therefore central to interpretability, reliability, and safe deployment.

Related research remains scattered across communities with different concepts, methods, and evaluation standards. This survey follows knowledge through one lifecycle, connecting training, internal representation, inference-time use, post-training intervention, transfer, and evaluation.

Treating these stages as successive states of one underlying object makes their dependencies visible and narrows the distance between behavioral observation and mechanistic understanding.

Unified taxonomy

The field at a glance

The survey organizes representative methods, findings, and benchmarks under the six stages of the knowledge lifecycle.

Lifecycle sections

Six views of model knowledge

Each section studies a distinct transformation of knowledge while tracing how it depends on earlier stages and changes the stages that follow.

01 / 06

Knowledge Acquisition

知识获取

Training methods / Training data / Model architecture

How knowledge enters a model, and how training strategy, corpus composition, architecture, and scale determine what can later be represented and used.

02 / 06

Knowledge Representation

知识表征

Parametric storage / Localization / Activation space

How acquired knowledge is encoded in model parameters and activation spaces, how it is organized, and how knowledge-bearing components can be localized.

03 / 06

Knowledge Utilization

知识利用

Knowledge recall / Augmentation / Conflict

How models access parametric knowledge, augment it with demonstrations, retrieval, and tools, and resolve conflicts among internal and external sources.

04 / 06

Knowledge Evolution

知识演化

Knowledge editing / Unlearning / Side effects

How outdated or undesirable knowledge is updated or removed after training, and how completeness, containment, and durability reveal intervention side effects.

05 / 06

Knowledge Transfer

知识迁移

Cross-model / Cross-modal / Cross-lingual

How knowledge crosses boundaries between models, modalities, and languages through behavioral signals, shared representations, and parameter-space transfer.

06 / 06

Knowledge Evaluation

知识评测

Factuality / Compositionality / Currency / Calibration

How parametric and contextual knowledge are measured across complementary dimensions, and how evaluation follows every stage of the lifecycle.
