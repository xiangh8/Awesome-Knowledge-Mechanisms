The organizing idea

One object, six connected stages

Knowledge is not a static store. It enters a model, takes internal form, drives behavior, changes over time, crosses boundaries, and must be measured throughout.

Why a lifecycle view

From observed behavior to internal mechanism

Research on knowledge in large language models is spread across interpretability, training, retrieval, model editing, unlearning, transfer, and evaluation. Studying these topics separately hides how an intervention at one stage changes outcomes elsewhere.

This survey treats knowledge as one underlying object under continuous transformation. The resulting lifecycle makes dependencies visible and helps connect behavioral observations with mechanistic explanations.
